## What's new in v0.1.0

The first version, for trying out with real courses before it is shared more widely.

- **Choose one course or several.** The Course Picker from Canvas Extractor Tools, with checkboxes,
  "every course in this term", several pasted course addresses, and saved groups.
- **Get ready for teaching.** Enter the term dates, then move due dates, set module unlock dates,
  add dates to module titles and insert holiday headers, in every selected course at once.
- **Find and replace.** Rename or renumber an assignment everywhere it's mentioned, swap old links
  and videos for new ones, or change any text, across every selected course.
- **Match rubrics to assignments.** Every assignment with its rubric in one list; link, swap or
  remove rubrics.
- **Recent changes, with undo.** Everything the app changed, with a backup of each change.

Every tool shows each change side by side before anything is saved, leaves alone anything edited
in Canvas after the preview, and checks each change in Canvas afterwards.
